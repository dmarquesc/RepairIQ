GPU MLE / Thermal

Prioritize threshold failures and correlate heartbeat and power symptoms before recommending replacement.

Verify configuration, reseat the device, retest, then isolate the card or slot before replacement.

Identify model and configuration first. Firmware actions require version validation and qualified approval.

Review required Rule HGX-FW-003
